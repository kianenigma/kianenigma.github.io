I feel like Youtube is not like I how I used to remember it even 5 years ago. Back then, my feed was curated, AI-generated videos were non-existent, and I was every day learning new things on Youtube, and truly educational channel's like [Crash Course](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse) and [Kurtzgesagt](https://www.youtube.com/@kurzgesagt) were the most popular. I also used to follow some original creators on Youtube ([1](https://www.youtube.com/@GeographyNow), [2](https://www.youtube.com/@ozzymanreviews), [3](https://www.youtube.com/@ElectroBOOM)) that I find ever more rare.

This page is my archive of the part of Youtube that I loved, before its [enshittifaction](http://enshittification.urbanup.com/17678793) [thanks to AI](https://youtu.be/-Gnrp_caPvo) and the algorithms.
